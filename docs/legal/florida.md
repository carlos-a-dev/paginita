# Florida reference for applicable deployments

This is a jurisdiction-specific reference from the original site audit, not a
policy or configuration applied to every site hosted by this CMS. Site operators
must determine applicability and obtain legal review before relying on it.

Florida Statutes §501.171 generally requires affected-individual notice within
30 days after determination or reason to believe a qualifying breach occurred,
subject to statutory exceptions. Department of Legal Affairs notification applies
when 500 or more Florida individuals are affected, and additional credit-reporting
notice applies when more than 1,000 individuals require notice at once. Third-party
agents generally have a 10-day notification deadline to the covered entity.
Ordinary names and email addresses alone do not necessarily meet its definition
of personal information; free-text messages may contain qualifying information.

Florida §501.204 prohibits unfair or deceptive commercial practices, including
potentially misleading privacy promises. The lack of a cookie-consent banner is
not automatically a Florida violation. The Digital Bill of Rights' controller
requirements are narrow, including a revenue threshold and other criteria;
check scope rather than assuming its full notice requirements apply.
